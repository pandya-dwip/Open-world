**Changes made:** meshes simplified, textures resized and converted to WebP, spec/gloss
materials converted to metal/rough, individual objects extracted from packs and scenes,
the mountain mirrored to form a full peak, the sand texture cropped from the dune model, houses and tree houses extracted from
their dioramas (ground planes, water and props removed), ship parts merged, unlit scan
materials (waterfalls, cloudy mountain) converted to lit ones.
These optimised kits are generated by `npm run build:env` from the original downloads in
`Assets/environment-source/`.

## Downloads not used

| File | Reason |
|---|---|
| rocks_photogrammetry_scan.glb (GSXNet) | CC BY-NC 4.0 (non-commercial only), and its baked "unlit" material cannot react to the day-night lighting |
| rigged__animated_weeping_willows.glb (Jagobo) | about 760k triangles of skinned meshes; the leaves did not render correctly |
| trees.glb (Helindu) | about 950k triangles of mostly bare / dead trees that do not suit the green island |
| tree_animate.glb (RandyGF) | sideways-oriented animated tree in the deprecated spec/gloss format; duplicates the broadleaf trees already used |
| mountain_terrain_-_haytor_dartmoor_national_park.glb (Chris Bibb) | about 1M triangles with a baked unlit scan texture; the island already has its mountain and the cloudy-mountain landmark |
| the_landscape_is_a_forest_in_the_mountains.glb (dasy444) | Sketchfab Standard licence (not CC); its trees are fused into one landscape mesh and cannot be separated |
| more_realistic_trees_free_v2.glb (Nicholas-3D) | a duplicate of More Realistic Trees Free!, which is already used |
| 3d_scan_of_a_path_to_the_beach.glb (christianmilan_) | tried as a trailhead marker at the beach entries, but the scan is a bumpy lump that the character sank into and that looked broken up close |
| puddled_mud_with_moss_-_pbr_seamless.glb (-Arthur-) | was the walking-path texture; the paths were removed at your request to gain frame rate |
